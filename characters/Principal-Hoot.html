Description

Principal Hoot is the head of Beringstern University, a school that has existed since 2002.

The player recently graduated from the university in Chapter 1, having completed their studies in 2024.

As of 2026, Principal Hoot has worked at Beringstern University for over twenty years and remains deeply passionate about teaching.

During Chapter 1, she offers the player the Professor job, one of the first four professions available.

Despite being a nocturnal animal, she enjoys the sunlight and is usually active during the day. She also enjoys reading, writing, and playing chess.

Principal Hoot is voiced by Mayvee.

FAQ

What made you want to become a chancellor?

I was actually once a professor in my early twenties at another town's college.

When Beringstern University opened in 2002, I moved here and joined the faculty. I've remained here ever since, watching generations of students grow and succeed.

What makes you a good chancellor?

Definitely a strong memory, years of experience, and the fact that I genuinely want to help students.
