BATTLE INFORMATION

Blightbot A is the first enemy you will fight in Chapter 1, so you may feel a little unsure of what to do. Not to worry though. Use what you learned, and you shall continue safely.

ATTACKS

Blightbot A will attack with a blue beam with good range. This is the only attack it has, and simply walking out of the way will do.

SPARE

At first glance you may not see it, but Blightbot A has a secret switch behind it. Just sneak up behind its back and stay there. Once the bot has turned fully green, it has been spared!
