About

Bubbleblight is the main antagonist of the Beringstern series and a self-proclaimed genius mastermind.

With countless plans, inventions, and questionable ideas, Bubbleblight believes he is the only person qualified to lead Beringstern.

During the events of Chapter 1, Bubbleblight kidnaps Mayor Ben and takes him to a private peninsula, hoping to finally take control of the town.

Unfortunately for Bubbleblight, a group of heroes decides to interfere with his perfect plan.

Bubbleblight is voiced by Cee the Bubble.
