About

Mayor Ben serves as the founder and mayor of Beringstern, a town built on cooperation, kindness, and opportunity.

During the events of Chapter 1, Mayor Ben is abducted by the infamous Bubbleblight, leading to a chain of events that affects the entire town.

Mayor Ben has never been rich before. He would try to get the best gifts for Christmas, but they never worked. Now, he can enjoy Christmas as the new mayor he is.

Mayor Ben is voiced by Parag S.

FAQ

What made you want to become mayor?

For a long time, Bubbles were not really treated fairly. Bubbles deserve as much respect as others. That thought eventually led to the founding of Beringstern.

Seeing other counties and towns become more welcoming because of one of my faithful actions made me really happy, and I couldn't imagine seeing the road ahead as a child.

Fun Facts
